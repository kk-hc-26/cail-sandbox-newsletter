Platform Updates
New
Second memos now return at least one macro node
Maps generated from a second memo include at least one macro node beside their exo nodes, so students begin from a structure rather than a list of leaves.
Improved
Policy language is read more precisely
Systems Mapping counts a policy as operative only when it obliges or forbids. Acronyms are read as organizations, and anything that sits outside an office is placed at exo.
Improved
Hand-mapping takes one double-click at every level
On the canvas, a double-click adds a node; on a node, it connects one. Clicks keep reaching the map until the student presses Cancel, so a sequence of placements no longer needs a mode switch.
New
Immediate settings are drawn into the map
When a student chooses an immediate setting for a micro node in its panel, the map now draws a line to that setting instead of holding it as text only.
Improved
Steps open in order, with a corner guide and Auto-fit
Steps now open in sequence beside a corner guide, and an Auto-fit switch returns the map to view without manual zooming.

Why this matters: Systems Mapping now sits beside Stem Splitter on the Tools portal, sharing one CUNY Login, reporting, and class progress.

Tool Spotlight
Systems Mapping
Concept mapping for policy and systems coursework, now on the Tools portal.

Systems Mapping is a concept-mapping workspace for policy and systems coursework. Students build maps by hand or generate them from second memos, and instructors see maps, reports, and class progress in the same place as their other tools. This release widens generated maps, tightens how policy language is read, and reduces hand-mapping to a double-click.
