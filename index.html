Formatting help and house style
Body fields accept light inline formatting: **bold**, *italic*, `code`, and [label](https://…). Everything else is escaped, so stray < characters are safe.

Voice and style: plain declarative sentences. No exclamation points, no emoji, no hype adjectives. State what changed and why it matters to the reader's work. Prefer concrete numbers and dates over "recently" and "soon".

Sending: download both the HTML and the .txt, and send them as multipart/alternative. Test in Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook first.
